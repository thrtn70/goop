№ 01 · The work

Four jobs. One window.

  1. 01

    Save it from a link.

    Paste a URL, choose an available format, and download a video, audio track, or image collection.

    YouTube, Vimeo, SoundCloud, and more. Need a signed-in session?

  2. 02

    Give it a new format.

    MOV to MP4. WAV to MP3. PNG to WebP. Choose a format for one file or a whole batch.

    Video, audio, images, and PDFs. Goop shows targets for the file you drop in.

  3. 03

    Make room to share.

    Reduce video, audio, image, or PDF size. Choose a quality setting, or a target size where available.

    You choose the trade-off between size and quality.

  4. 04

    Get PDFs in order.

    Combine documents, split out the pages you need, or compress a PDF before sending it.

    PDF jobs share the same queue as your media.

The useful detailsQueue, shortcuts & more
Queue & history
Follow progress, reorder jobs, and find finished files in History. Reveal an output in Finder with one click.
Pause & resume
Paused downloads keep partial files across restarts. Paused encodes can resume while the app stays open.
Encoding choices
Goop can use VideoToolbox on supported Mac hardware. Compatible formats can be repackaged without re-encoding, a process called remuxing.
Keyboard shortcuts
⌘ K opens the command palette. ⌘ N adds a URL; ⌘ O opens files; ⌘ , opens settings.
Accessibility
Keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader labels, and reduced-motion support.

№ 02 · The setup

Your first launch.

One extra macOS step. Then you're in.

  1. 01

    Download & install.

    Download the macOS installer, open the .dmg, and drag Goop to Applications. Requires macOS 13+ and Apple Silicon.

  2. 02

    Allow the first launch.

    Open Goop once. If macOS blocks it because it can't verify the developer, open System Settings → Privacy & Security. Choose Open Anyway next to Goop, then confirm.

    Goop is ad-hoc signed, but isn't Apple-notarized yet. The option appears after the blocked attempt.

    If macOS says Goop “is damaged”

    For a copy downloaded from this project's GitHub Releases, run this once in Terminal, then open Goop again. It removes extended attributes, including quarantine, from that copy of the app.

    sudo xattr -cr /Applications/Goop.app
  3. 03

    Bring your first file.

    Paste a link in Extract or drop a file into Convert. Pick your output, then start. ⌘ K gets you around.

№ 03 · The questions

Frequently raised.

  1. i. Is Goop really free and open-source?

    Yes, MIT licensed, and the source is on GitHub. No account, subscription, or paid tier.

  2. ii. Why is macOS blocking the app on first launch?

    Goop isn't Apple-notarized yet. Follow the first-launch guide for the verification warning or the “is damaged” message.

  3. iii. Where is the Windows build?

    Windows builds are on hold while development focuses on macOS. Past release notes remain available for reference.

  4. iv. A download needs a login, or stopped working.

    For content you can access in your browser, choose that browser under Settings → General → Cookies from browser. Goop reads the session locally; it doesn't upload your cookies.

    A 403 can also mean a site changed or blocked the request. Check for downloader updates in Settings → Updates, or report the problem. Support depends on the site.

  5. v. Where do my downloads go?

    Wherever you point Output folder in Settings › General. From History, hit the folder icon on a completed item to show it in Finder.

  6. vi. Does Goop send any of my data anywhere?

    Conversions run locally. Goop doesn't upload your files or collect telemetry. Downloads contact the sites you choose. Optional update checks contact GitHub; a manual gallery-dl update contacts Codeberg. Control updates in Settings → Updates.
